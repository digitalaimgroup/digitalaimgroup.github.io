Big Tyme Ballaz Support

For parents and guardians: contact digitalaimgroup@gmail.com for help. Include your device model, app version and a description of the issue. Please do not send personal information about a child.

Getting started

Complete the app tour, then open Today to start a workout. Explore Drills for demonstrations and Progress for workout history and skill tests.

Progress and settings

Progress is stored on the device. Use Settings → Reset my progress to clear it. Resetting removes saved progress, unlocked levels and skill-test scores.

For parents

The app has no accounts, ads, tracking or purchases. External links and support email are behind an adult math check.
