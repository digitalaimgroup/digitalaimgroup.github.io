Big Tyme Ballaz
Privacy Policy

App by Digital AIM · Effective November 1, 2026

The short version for parents:
  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • We don't collect your child's name, email, location, photos, voice or any other personal information.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no ads in this version.
  • Workout progress is saved only on your child's device.
  • Links that leave the app, like this page or emailing us, are behind a parent check.

Who we are

Big Tyme Ballaz is an at-home basketball training app made by Digital AIM in Dallas, Texas. It's made for young players. Questions about this policy: digitalaimgroup@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app doesn't ask for, collect or send any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way for players to message each other.

What stays on the device

To show streaks and progress, the app saves a small amount of workout data only on the device:

  • Which days a workout was finished, the current streak, points (XP) and badges
  • Which training level is unlocked and picked
  • Free-throw scores the player taps in (makes and shots)
  • Skill-test scores the player types in (crossovers in 60 seconds and 5-spot shooting)
  • Settings: shooting hand (right or left), whether countdown beeps are on, and whether the first-time tour was seen

This data never leaves the device. We can't see it, and it isn't shared with anyone. Delete it any time with Settings → Reset my progress, or by deleting the app.

Permissions

The app doesn't use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location. It doesn't use the advertising ID. It only plays its own demo videos and short beep sounds.

Analytics, ads and third parties

This version has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no ads and no third-party tracking tools, and it shares no data with third parties. The team leaderboard in this version shows example players only.

If we ever add ads, they will be non-personalized, kid-safe ads from a network certified for Google Play's Families program, shown only after a workout, and any ad tap will ask for a parent first. We'll update this policy before that happens.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The app is made for kids, so we built it to collect no personal information from children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. A parent check (a simple math question for adults) stands in front of anything that leaves the app, such as links, email or any future purchase.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and change the date at the top. If a change would mean collecting any personal information, we'll get a parent's consent first, as the law requires.
